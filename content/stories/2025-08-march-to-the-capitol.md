On 7 August 2025 thousands of Liberians marched under one banner: "Liberia Says No To Drugs." Their message was "Protect our youth, save our future."

The march was a national statement against the rise and spread of illicit drug use in Liberia. We marched with our affiliate organisations: MOTARWY, West Africa Drug Policy Network, LIBxRecords Foundation, EMSASA, Justice Forum of Liberia and GLHF. They organised and mobilised the day, and stood in the rain with thousands of citizens to ask for change.

That evening we thanked the organisers and everyone who came, and said the work does not end with one march. We called for a lasting, nationwide effort: "community by community, school by school, street by street."

On 18 November 2025 we took that message into a school. [Read about the visit](/stories/2025-11-school-visit/).
