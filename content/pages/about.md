## Why "We The People"

In a democracy the people put governments in power and the people remove them. The people suffer first when government fails and benefit first when it works. Everything comes back to the people.

## What we are for

We want the country's attention to move from politics to development. We are building a neutral round table where people of every party, faith, profession, ethnicity and background work together on the issues that affect ordinary people: drugs and recovery, skills, financing, and community networks in every county.

## How we work

- Only love, no hate.
- Only peace, no aggression.
- Only partners, no enemies.
- Responsibility is shared. We focus on solutions, not blame.
- People take charge of their own future.
