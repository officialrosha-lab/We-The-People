## Who we are

We The People Movement is a Liberian civic movement. Its legal name is **We The People, Inc.** It brings people of every party, faith, profession and background to one neutral table to work on what affects ordinary people: drugs and recovery, skills in every county, access to financing, and community branches.

We are not a political party. See [About](/about/) and [Neutrality](/about/#neutrality).
