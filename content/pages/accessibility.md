**Draft, written 2026-10-01.** We want everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard instead of a mouse, a small or low-end phone, or a slow connection.

## The standard we aim for

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

## What we have done

- Every page can be used with a keyboard alone. A "Skip to content" link comes first, and the focus outline is always visible.
- Text and controls meet the contrast ratios in the guidelines.
- Photographs have written descriptions. Decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
- The opening animation on the home page plays once, can be skipped by pressing any key or clicking, and does not play at all if your device is set to reduce motion. The page headline and the list of all fifteen counties are there from the start.
- Forms have visible labels and say what is wrong and how to fix it. They do not use a CAPTCHA.
- The site works without JavaScript for reading, and is light enough for slow connections.
- Pages are tested automatically for accessibility problems every time the site changes.

## What is not yet right

- **The videos (the introduction on the home page and the speech on the 7 August 2025 march story) have no captions.** People who cannot hear them cannot follow the speech. Each page describes what its video shows. We intend to add captions.
