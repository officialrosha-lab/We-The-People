## What this website does not do

- It sets no cookies.
- It has no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
- It loads its fonts, images and scripts from its own address, not from other companies.
- It remembers one thing in your browser, for that visit only: that you have already seen the opening animation on the home page, so it does not play again.

## What we collect, and when

We collect information only when you send it to us through a form on this site.

- **Join the movement:** your name, an email address or phone number, your county, and what you would like to help with.
- **Contact:** your name, an email address or phone number, and your message.
- **Event registration:** your name, an email address or phone number, and your county, for the event you choose.
- **Get updates:** your email address.

## Who handles it

Two outside services receive what you send, on our behalf:

- **Formspree** receives the join, contact and event forms and passes them to our inbox.
- **Brevo** receives newsletter sign-ups and sends the emails. It asks you to confirm your address before you are added, and every email has an unsubscribe link.
