- Why the name: in a democracy the driving force is the people. The people put governments in power, remove them, suffer first under bad government, and benefit from good government and a stable nation.
- Stage when recorded (historical): **planning stage**, with daily meetings, starting with willing young people. Date of the interview is unknown **[VERIFY]**.

## Mission (in the speaker's words, paraphrased)
Move the country's attention from politics to development, by creating a neutral round table where people of every party, religion, profession, ethnicity, and economic background work together on the issues that affect ordinary people.

## Stance and values
- Not a political party. A neutral space. Members are not asked to leave their party.
- Engage the government and the opposition as partners; do not fight them. "We only have partners. We don't have enemies."
- Shared tagline candidates from the interview: "Only love, no hate. Only peace, no aggression. Only partners, no enemies." and the idea of "peace, love, and understanding" **[VERIFY wording]**.
- Shared responsibility: "It's all of us" who brought the country here; focus on solutions rather than blame.
- People should take charge, because no one else will stabilize the country for them.

## Priority issues
1. **Drugs (cocaine and others)**: called a national urgency. Claims of over 100,000 people with addiction and a single rehabilitation center with capacity around 40 **[VERIFY all numbers and the location, which was unclear]**. Calls for rehabilitation centers across the country, community-level reporting of where drugs are, and contributions of money, time, and effort to help government respond.
2. **Vocational schools in every county**, so young people gain marketable skills. Counties named include Grand Kru, River Gee, and Lofa; other names were garbled **[VERIFY]**.
3. Access to **loans and financing** and technical capacity for ordinary people.

## How it intends to work
- Branches and sub-branches across communities, universities, and counties, linked as a network.
- Government, the people, the international community, and Friends of Liberia named as partners.
- Contributions of members' own resources, money, time, and community effort.

## Membership and calls to action
- Membership is open to everybody; the membership drive was described as ongoing, with a public launch "very soon." Framed as "we're not recruiting; people gravitate to it."
- Audiences addressed: young people, older people who want to correct past mistakes, the international community, Friends of Liberia, and the media.
- The media's contribution: free air time and publicity for programs.

## What this means for the website
- Likely audiences: Liberians at home across counties, the diaspora, young people, partners and international supporters, media.
- Primary actions: **join / become a member**, **start or join a local branch**, **volunteer**, **support** (donations, in-kind, media partnership), **report and learn about drug-related issues in communities** (design carefully for safety).
- Strong content pillars: Our Why (the name), Our Principles (neutral, partners not enemies), Drug Response, Skills and Vocational Education, Community Branches, Join.
- Design implications: the brand must feel neutral and inclusive, not aligned with any party colors or symbols. Check party color associations in Liberia before choosing the palette.
