# 12 — Open Questions (answer before or during Phase 0)

1. Where is We The People registered and where does it operate? (Determines payment providers, legal text, tax receipts.)
2. What is the one-sentence mission, and what is the story behind the name?
3. Which programs, campaigns, and causes should the site feature at launch?
4. What verified impact numbers and real stories can be published, with consent?
5. Does a brand identity exist (logo, colors, fonts), or should one be designed?
6. Which payment methods can the organization actually accept?
7. Which languages must the site support at launch?
8. Which tools are already in use (email list, CRM, accounting, social)?
9. Who will edit content after launch, and how technical are they?
10. What is the launch date and the budget for ongoing hosting and services?
11. Are there organizations or sites admired, and what specifically is admired?
12. Is the organization eligible for nonprofit programs (e.g. Google Ad Grants, cloud and software discounts)?
13. Any sensitive contexts (political, safeguarding, safety of participants) that affect imagery, naming, or data collection?

14. The logo reads "We The People Movement". Is that the official full name?
