# Copy and Microcopy

Words are design content. Each element does one job. Write from the visitor's point of view, plain language, active voice, sentence case, no filler. Everything below is **DRAFT for owner approval**. Do not invent facts, numbers or quotes. Use `[PLACEHOLDER: ...]` where content is missing and log it in `content/PLACEHOLDERS.md`.

## Buttons and links (consistent verbs)
- Join the movement
- Give [only when payments are enabled]
- Read the full story
- Find your county
- Add to calendar
- Send

The same action keeps the same name from button to confirmation (for example "Join the movement" leads to "You have joined").

## Forms
- Labels: Name, Email or phone, County, How do you want to help?
- Helper under county: "Pick the county where you live. Pick 'Outside Liberia' if you live abroad."
- Success: "You have joined the movement. We will contact you at [email or phone] within [PLACEHOLDER: days]."
- Error examples (explain what and how to fix, no apology):
  - "Enter an email address or a phone number so we can reach you."
  - "That phone number has too few digits. Check it and try again."
  - "We could not send your form. Check your connection and press Send again."

## Empty and error states
- No events: "No events are scheduled. Check back soon or join to hear first."
- 404: "This page does not exist. Go to the home page or find your county."
- Offline: "You are offline. Pages you opened before are still available."

## Voice
Direct, warm, specific, and calm. Peaceful and lawful in tone. Dignified toward every person. Avoid: "making a difference," "empowering," "change-makers," "journey," "passionate about."
