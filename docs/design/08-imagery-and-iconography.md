# Imagery and Iconography

## Photography
- Real people and real places in Liberia: community meetings, branch activities, young people learning skills, markets, streets, rural and urban counties. Natural light, documentary, honest.
- Show people as agents with dignity. Avoid pity imagery and posed handshake photos. No stock.
- Consent recorded in `content/CONSENT-LOG.md`. Credit photographers. Do not publish faces of people who could be put at risk; ask.
- Treatment: full-color, uncolored, no duotone or gradient overlay. Color lives in type and blocks.
- Technical: responsive `srcset`, AVIF/WebP with JPEG fallback, explicit width/height, meaningful alt text, lazy-load below the fold. Provide low-data variants.

## The logo
- Use the vector version once available (`docs/01b`). Provide: full-color on light, full-color on dark, one-color, icon-only (figures + flag), favicon.
- Place the full logo only in the footer and on the About page. The header uses the icon-only mark.
- Do not tint or recolor the logo; do not place it on busy photos.

## Iconography
- A small set of simple pictograms derived from the logo's three figures and star. Use sparingly as section markers only where they carry meaning (for example one marker for "Join").
- Icons are inline SVG, 2px stroke or solid, aria-hidden when decorative.

## Illustration and pattern
- Optional: a restrained pattern from the logo's star and flag stripes, used on one surface only (for example the footer base) at low contrast, never as wallpaper.

## Media
- Video: captions and transcript required; `preload="none"`; poster frame; no autoplay with sound.
- Audio (radio clips, voices): transcript required.
