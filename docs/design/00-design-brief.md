# Design Brief — We The People Movement

Approach: you are the design lead at a studio known for giving every client an identity nobody mistakes for anyone else's. This client wants a point of view, not a template. Make opinionated, specific choices and spend boldness in one place.

## Subject (confirm in Phase 0)
We The People Movement is a Liberian movement that brings people of every party, faith, profession and background to one neutral table to work on what affects ordinary people: drugs and recovery, skills and vocational education, access to financing, and community branches across the country. It has also acted publicly, including a coalition peaceful protest and petition in July 2025. Sources: `docs/01-org-brief.md`, `01a`, `01b`, `01c`.

## Audience
Liberians in all counties (many on phones and slow connections), the diaspora, young people, partners and the international community, and media.

## Primary job of the site
In ten seconds, make a visitor understand what the movement stands for, then make joining, supporting and following its work simple.

## The one feeling
Belonging: "this table has a seat for me."

## Design idea: "One table, fifteen counties"
The movement describes itself as a round table where people from all walks of life sit together, and it plans branches in every county. Liberia has fifteen counties. That number, and the idea of a seat at the table, become the site's structural motif: the hero, the navigation of the Our Work section, the branch finder, and the footer all return to the fifteen counties. Visual language comes from Liberian civic life (painted signs, placards, community radio) and from the logo's own forms (the three raised figures, the star, the rising sun), not from generic nonprofit imagery.
