# 06 — Content and Voice

## Voice
Defined by the organization in `docs/01` (five adjectives). Until then: direct, human, specific, hopeful without being saccharine.

## Writing rules
- Plain words. Name things by what visitors understand, not how the organization is structured.
- Active voice. Sentence case. No filler.
- Be specific: who, where, how many, since when. Cite the source and date for every number.
- Buttons say exactly what happens: "Give monthly," "Join the next clean-up," "Sign the petition." The same action keeps the same name through the whole flow.
- Errors explain what went wrong and how to fix it. They do not apologize or joke.
- Avoid: "making a difference," "empowering," "change-makers," "journey," "impact" as a vague noun, "passionate about."
- Dignity first: describe people by what they do and want, not only by what they lack.
- Short paragraphs, descriptive subheads, line length under ~75 characters.

## Story structure (for each story)
1. A specific person and moment
2. The obstacle, stated plainly
3. What We The People and the community did
4. What changed, with a verifiable detail
5. A clear way for the reader to act

## Content rules
- Consent is documented for every person, photo, and quote. Track in `content/CONSENT-LOG.md`.
- No placeholder statistics. Use `[PLACEHOLDER: ...]` and list in `content/PLACEHOLDERS.md`.
- Every page has a meta description (under 160 characters) and one clear primary action.

## Hero and homepage copy brief
Open with the most characteristic thing in this organization's world, not a slogan. Candidates to explore: a statement of purpose, a real voice, a place, a live number, an interactive moment. Propose three options with rationale before choosing.
