# 09 — Legal and Compliance (scaffolds for human or legal review)

Claude Code drafts structure only. A qualified person must review before launch.

- **Privacy policy**: what data is collected, why, retention, processors, rights, contact. Align with the laws of the organization's country and of major donor regions (GDPR/UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and local law).
- **Cookie and consent**: only if tracking beyond strictly necessary is used; prefer cookieless analytics.
- **Donation terms**: refund policy, recurring-gift cancellation, how funds are used, tax-deductibility language that matches the organization's real legal status.
- **Registration disclosures**: legal name, registration number, address, as required by local charity or fundraising regulators.
- **Terms of use**, **accessibility statement**, **safeguarding and child-protection policy** (if working with minors), **whistleblower and complaints policy** link.
- **Payments**: use hosted fields or provider-hosted checkout; never store card data. Follow the provider's compliance requirements.
- **Content rights**: licenses for fonts, images, music; photographer credits; consent log in `content/CONSENT-LOG.md`.
- **Email**: double opt-in, one-click unsubscribe, sender identification.
- **Children's data**: do not collect from minors without guardian consent.
