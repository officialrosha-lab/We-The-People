# 01c — Public Record and Positioning (confirmed by the owner)

The owner confirmed that the public news reports found about the "We The People" movement are accurate. Facts below come from published news coverage (AllAfrica and others, July 2025). Re-check each against the primary source before publishing anything on the site.

## Confirmed public history
- In July 2025 the We The People Movement worked with the civil-society coalition **STAND (Solidarity and Trust for a New Day)** and other grassroots groups.
- They notified the Ministry of Justice of a **nationwide, three-day peaceful protest, July 15 to 17, 2025**, citing the constitutional right to peaceful assembly (Article 17 of the 1986 Constitution).
- Stated concerns in coverage: worsening living conditions, governance and corruption, removal of security officials accused of abuse, and restrictions affecting motorcycle and tricycle operators.
- Coverage of the July 17 protest describes a **petition** presented to the government and a **14-day ultimatum** for acknowledgment, with former CDC chairman **Mulbah Morlu** speaking for the organizers.
- The CDC Youth League publicly endorsed the July 17 protest.

## Resolved: one movement, two chapters (owner confirmed)
The radio interview is the movement's **founding statement** (neutral, non-party, partnership, development). The 2025 protests and petition are a **later chapter** of the same movement. The site should tell it as one story: **who we are and why (founding), what we have done (record), and what we are doing now (current work)**. Positioning option 3 ("both") is the natural frame, subject to the owner's final wording.

## What this means
The movement has a visible **advocacy and accountability** history, and the radio interview presents a **neutral, non-party, development-focused** identity (drugs, vocational schools, partnership with government). These can coexist, but the website must say clearly how. Do not leave visitors to guess.

Open positioning choice (owner decides, see `docs/12`):
1. **Civic-development movement** that engages everyone, with advocacy as one tool
2. **Accountability movement** led by petitions and public action, with development programs as the constructive side
3. **A deliberate "both"**: "We hold power accountable and we build" with a clear structure for each

## Consequences for the website
- **Neutrality statement** written explicitly: how the movement relates to parties, coalitions, and endorsements. Avoid imagery that suggests allegiance to any party.
- **Accuracy and sourcing**: a timeline of actions with dates and links to independent coverage; no unverified claims about officials or institutions. Legal review for any named individuals.
- **Peaceful, lawful framing** throughout the voice and content (see `docs/06`), consistent with the movement's own stated principles.
- **Funding transparency**: clear statement of who funds the movement, since political and advocacy groups attract scrutiny.
- **Media and rapid response**: a press room and a "latest statements" feed that staff can update in minutes.
- **Resilience**: static pages cached on a CDN, a mirror or fallback status page, and low-bandwidth versions for areas with weak connectivity.
