# 02 — Audiences, Goals, Success Metrics

## Primary audiences (confirm or edit)
| Audience | What they need | What we want them to do |
|---|---|---|
| Potential donors | Trust, clarity on where money goes, easy giving | Give once, then monthly |
| Volunteers / members | What to do, when, where, how to start | Sign up, join a chapter or event |
| Beneficiaries / communities served | Plain-language help, how to reach the org | Contact, apply, attend |
| Advocates / supporters | Issues explained, simple actions | Sign, share, contact representatives |
| Partners / funders / sponsors | Credibility, impact data, governance | Start a conversation, download a deck |
| Press | Facts, assets, spokespeople | Use the press kit, contact |
| Job and internship seekers | Roles and culture | Apply |

## Site goals (ordered)
1. Make a first-time visitor understand who We The People is and why it matters within 10 seconds.
2. Convert attention into action (donate, volunteer, subscribe, sign).
3. Build trust through transparency (governance, finances, real stories).
4. Give staff self-service control of content without developers.
5. Earn recognition for design and craft.

## Success metrics
- Donation conversion rate, average gift, recurring-gift share
- Volunteer and newsletter sign-ups per 1,000 visits
- Event registrations
- Core Web Vitals pass rate; accessibility audit score
- Scroll depth and time on key story pages
- Awards submitted and results (Awwwards, CSS Design Awards, FWA, Webby, Communication Arts)

## Context to design for
- Mobile-first, potentially low bandwidth and older devices
- Visitors with disabilities (screen readers, low vision, cognitive load, motion sensitivity)
- Possibly multilingual audiences; plan for i18n even if launching in one language
